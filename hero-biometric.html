About The Service

Empowering Assessments with Intelligent Biometric Solutions

Our Mobile & Tablet Based Biometric Service transforms Android devices into powerful biometric workstations capable of performing attendance management, employee verification, student enrollment, candidate authentication, and workforce tracking without relying on expensive fixed biometric terminals.

Our solution supports seamless integration with existing enterprise systems while providing real-time data synchronization, centralized monitoring, and enhanced operational control.

Whether you manage thousands of employees across multiple locations or conduct large-scale examination processes, our biometric services deliver accuracy, speed, and reliability at every stage.
